## On tic80.com

The site moved on too:

- **Games delete and restore.** Your own cartridges, from their page or your
  profile: hidden at once, recoverable for 30 days, comments and +1s included.
  An account can delete itself the same way.
- **A notifications page** — comments and +1s on your cartridges, with a
  counter in the navigation.
- **A liked-games page** — everything you have +1'd, in one place.
- **+1 is open to everyone**: the old gate that required your own games to be
  liked first is gone.
- **Sign in with your e-mail** as well as your user name.
- **A full-screen player** — a game page, and the browser IDE, can open the
  cartridge alone: no site chrome, the player fills the screen, and the game
  starts as soon as the page opens.
- **Every game states the language** it is written in.
- **Game pages load a player built for that language** — several times smaller
  than the universal one, so they start faster.
- **New games are announced in the community chat** again.
